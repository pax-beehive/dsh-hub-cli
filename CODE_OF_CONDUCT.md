# Code of Conduct

**English** · [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive,
assume good intent, focus criticism on technical work, and respect privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Continuing disruptive or off-topic interaction after being asked to stop.
- Posting credentials, tokens, environment values, or other sensitive data.
- Other conduct that creates an unsafe environment for community members.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior threatens contributors or the community.
A maintainer involved in a report must recuse themselves.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[hello@dshpluginhub.ai](mailto:hello@dshpluginhub.ai). Maintainers will handle
reports confidentially and avoid conflicts of interest. Report vulnerabilities
through the private process in the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
